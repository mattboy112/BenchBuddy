    Safety

    When this is on, every output turns off if no browser has been connected for 10 seconds. Good for motors, heaters and anything you shouldn't leave running.

    The red All off button at the top always works, on every tab.

    Setups & configs

    Suspend pauses every output but keeps your whole setup, so Resume picks up right where you left off. Save a setup to recall it after a power-cycle, or export it to share as a file.

    WiFi setup

    Press Scan to find networks nearby.

    After it restarts, BenchBuddy joins this network. If it can't, its own hotspot comes back so you're never locked out.

    Firmware update

    Flash a new build over WiFi. In PlatformIO, build, then pick .pio/build/bench/firmware.bin.

    Outputs switch off during the update. The board restarts by itself when it's done.
